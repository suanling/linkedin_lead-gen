# Content Unit — Hook Retain Reward

**Idea/concept:** All audience-growing content is made of the same repeating unit: Hook → Retain → Reward.

**Definition:** A *content unit* is the smallest piece of content that hooks attention, retains it, and delivers a reward. It can be as small as a tweet, meme, or jingle — or as large as a book chapter. The key is that all three elements are present.

---

## The Three Components

### 1. Hook — Get them to notice
- **Goal:** Make them redirect attention toward you
- **Tools:** Topic (Far Past, Recent Past, Present, Trending, Manufactured) + Headline + Format matching the platform
- **Headline drivers** (use ≥ 2): Recency, Relevancy, Celebrity, Proximity, Conflict, Unusual, Ongoing
- **Measured by:** % of people who start consuming your content
- Effort investment: the first 5 seconds of any ad/post are worth 80% of the total. Focus there.

### 2. Retain — Keep them consuming
- **Goal:** Embed unanswered questions so they keep going
- **Best tools:** Lists, Steps, and Stories — because each embeds an implicit question ("what's next?")
- Curiosity is the primary retention mechanism. Unresolved questions create a loop that must be closed.

### 3. Reward — Satisfy the reason they started
- **Goal:** Deliver on the implicit or explicit promise of the hook
- A rewarded audience shares content and comes back for more — this is how audiences compound

---

## North — Where does it come from?

- Every format of content (podcast, reel, newsletter, LinkedIn post, tweet, ad) follows this structure whether the creator knows it or not
- The best-performing content on any platform reverse-engineers this pattern
- Related: [[Hooks — 3 Emotion Framework]] (the owner's skill for generating hooks using Admiration, Curiosity, Polarisation)

## West — What is it similar to?

- **AIDA** (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action) — similar skeleton, different framing
- **StoryBrand** (Donald Miller) — the hero's journey applied to marketing
- **Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS)** copywriting structure
- The hooks skill already in the vault mirrors the "Hook" component

## East — What is it opposite of?

- Information dumps — content that starts with the payload without earning attention first
- Rewarding content that never hooked (buried value no one consumes)
- Hooking content that never rewards (clickbait — destroys trust)

## South — Where does it lead to?

- Mastering this framework turns content into a lead-generating machine: audience grows → engaged leads grow → customers grow
- The content unit is the atom of [[Free Content as Advertising]]
- Combining hook + retain + reward across different formats and platforms is how you scale from one post to a media team
