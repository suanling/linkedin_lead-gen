# Creating Scroll-Stopping Hooks

After analysing 1000-s of viral content hooks, here is a simple yet effective way to create your very own hooks.

The opening hooks can significantly impact a content's performance. Even top creators like Justin Welsh spend most of their writing time on hooks.

## 2 Guidelines for Creating Hooks

### 1) Trigger at least one of these emotions:

- **Admiration** (E.g. I closed 100-s of high-ticket sales through cold calling.)
- **Curiosity** (E.g. After 10 years of cold calling, this is my #1 tip.)
- **Polarisation** (E.g. 90% will fail at cold calling.)

### 2) If possible, apply these refinements:

- **Quantify** (E.g. Here is how I sign new clients monthly. → Here is how I sign 4 to 8 new clients monthly.)
- **Personalise** (E.g. Use this tip to sign new clients monthly. → I use this tip to sign 4 to 8 new clients monthly.)
- **Simplify and shorten** (E.g. I am giving away my client acquisition system which can land you 4 to 8 new clients monthly. → FREE Giveaway: My sales system which can land you 4 to 8 new clients monthly.)
