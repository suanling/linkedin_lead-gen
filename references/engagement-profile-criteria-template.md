## How to read the markers

**Mark every criterion you fill in.** This is what stops a useful idea becoming an invented fact.

| | Means |
|---|---|
| ✅ | **Directly supported by the owner's own profile or their answers.** Quote-backed. |
| ⚙️ | **An operating decision.** Reasonable, chosen, and changeable. Not evidence. |
| 🔍 | **Needs live checking.** A PDF cannot settle it. Record `unverified` until checked. |

Never promote a ⚙️ to ✅ without a line in the owner's own material to point at. Anything the
owner has not decided goes in **Operating decisions to make** at the end, not into a group as if
it were settled.

## Compliance — who is commentable

> Delete this whole section if the owner holds no licence and works in no regulated field.

- The owner holds: [licence or regulated role, or "none"]
- ⛔ **Blocked accounts** — they ARE the regulated thing: [e.g. a currently licensed competitor]
- ✅ **Allowed** — they SELL TO that audience: [e.g. trainers and coaches serving that market]
- The exclusion follows the **venture**, not the person: someone can run an excluded business and
  a clean one. **But a venture-level admission does not survive a headline that advertises the
  excluded business.**
- **Post filter** for admitted accounts — comment on: [safe topics] · skip: [unsafe topics]

**Why two tests.** The QA gate reads the *draft* and never sees the post it sits under. A clean
comment beneath the wrong post still puts the owner's name in that thread. The gate is a text
rule; the filter is a placement rule.

## PDF Evidence Rules

### A profile PDF can show
- Name, location, headline, current roles, company, career history
- Founder or employee status where stated
- About text, services, stated clients, case studies, credentials
- Stated business type, audience, problems, and outcomes
- Concurrent roles and potential conflicts

### A profile PDF cannot prove
- Current posting frequency
- Actual audience composition
- Who comments on posts
- Revenue, team size, or client volume unless explicitly stated
- Whether the founder is truly a bottleneck unless explicitly described
- Whether they are commentable today

Use `unverified` instead of guessing.

---

## Rules across all groups

- **Test all three groups before rejecting a profile.** A profile that fails one may be strong in
  another, and stopping early is how good voices get lost.
- **"Employee", "not founder" and "product business" are Group 2 findings, not universal
  rejections.** Record the decline as `declined-G2` and name what Groups 1 and 3 returned.
- **A genuine buyer stays Group 2 even with a large audience.** Record the reach as an
  `audience-host` note; a person cannot be worked as both at once.

### Three separate things, often confused

| | Means | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualified target** | Commercially relevant, including quiet founders | `Status` |
| **Active account** | Posts or comments visibly, recently | `Activity status` |
| **Commentable today** | Has a workable post now, outside cooldown and company lane | `Commentable today` |

A qualified target need not be active. An active account need not be commentable today. Only the
third decides whether an account enters today's comment queue.

**Statuses:** `qualified-active` · `qualified-quiet` · `accepted-g1` · `peer-watch` ·
`research-needed` · `compliance-review` · `declined-g2` · `excluded-all`.
